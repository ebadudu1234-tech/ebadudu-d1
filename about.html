(ABOUT ME)

Warning: I Run on Toy Energy

Zihe Wang

↖ pick up a toy

_01

My shelves have always been full of toys. They have never been just toys to me. They are my friends, and every time I look at them, they give me a special, happy energy. When I could not find the part I wanted, I designed it myself and built it with a 3D printer.

_02

That energy led me to graphic design, and then to games. For my graduation project, I built a game and led a small team for the first time: two programmers, and I made all the art. I gave everyone the same tasks and the same time, but everyone has different skills, so it did not work well. Now I try to see what each person is good at, and I lead with more encouragement.

_03

A good toy does not need a manual to make someone smile. It speaks without a word. That is what I want from design. I like to make overwhelming things feel less overwhelming, and let them feel like play. When I start a project, I always think about how to turn it into something real, and I keep watching new design technology to find better ways to do it. My toys give me happy energy, and I want my work to give people the same energy.

_04

I hope everything I design earns a place on someone's shelf.
