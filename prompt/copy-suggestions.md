## 怎么打分

stop-slop 的 5 项，每项 1–10，满分 50，**低于 35 建议改**：

| 项 | 问的是 |
|---|---|
| 直接 Directness | 是直接说事，还是先"宣布"？ |
| 节奏 Rhythm | 句子长短有变化，还是一个调子？ |
| 信任 Trust | 相信读者，还是解释过头？ |
| 真实 Authenticity | 像人写的吗？ |
| 密度 Density | 有没有能删的？ |

常见问题：**"不是 X，是 Y"的对比套路**、副词（just / actually / exactly / always）、被动句、段落用一句短"金句"收尾、"So"开头、空泛的词（"in a technological way"）。

整体来说你的描述**已经很干净**：具体（材料、颜色、尺寸、引语都写出来了）、少形容词、没有破折号。大部分作品 37–41 分，不用动。下面只列**有具体问题的句子**。

### 5:50 Cafe（38 → 41，可选）

1. 原：*I wanted to change that.*
   改：*I wanted brown sugar to be the snack itself.*
   为什么：一句短句收尾、没说"change"成什么；改后说清楚目标。
2. 原：*It offers warm, balanced milk tea and small bites, not loud, over-sweet boba.*
   改：*It offers warm, balanced milk tea and small bites, with far less sugar than a boba shop.*
   为什么："X, not Y" 对比套路；直接说它是什么。
3. 原：*The logo is simple: a soft yellow circle …*
   改：*The logo is a soft yellow circle …*
   为什么："simple" 是在评价，后面的描述已经让人看到它简单。

### Switchback（34 → 41，建议）

1. 原：*They judge everything as a system, not as single products.*
   改：*They judge the gear as one system.*
2. 原：*So the missing thing is not more features. It is a harness the dog will actually wear.*
   改：*The missing piece is a harness the dog will wear.*
   为什么：典型的"不是 X。是 Y。"+ "actually" + "So" 开头。改后意思一样，更有力。

### Under the Mask（36 → 42，建议）

1. 原：*The main idea: the hero was shaped by cinematography.*
   改：*The main idea: cinematography shaped the hero.*
   为什么：被动句改主动。
2. 原：*… and the suit became not only a costume but a visual structure of silhouette, motion and camera.*
   改：*… and the suit became a visual structure of silhouette, motion and camera.*
   为什么："not only X but Y" 是凑出来的对比；"visual structure" 已经比"costume"多了。

### Lotus Cigarette（33 → 40，建议）

1. 原：*My project is a creative commercial for Lotus cigarettes, …*
   改：*I made a commercial for Lotus cigarettes, …*
   为什么："My project is a creative …" 是在宣布；"creative" 让作品自己证明。
2. 原：*The animation starts by showing the cigarette being built from tobacco, paper and a filter in a technological way.*
   改：*The animation opens with machines building the cigarette from tobacco, paper and a filter.*
   为什么："in a technological way" 很空，直接说是机器在造。被动"being built"改主动。
3. 原：*By putting an industrial object together with a natural and meaningful symbol, I want to give the brand a more elegant and unexpected image.*
   改：*Pairing a factory-made object with the lotus gives the brand a more elegant, unexpected image.*
   为什么："a natural and meaningful symbol" 绕了一圈，其实就是莲花；"I want to" 去掉，直接说结果。

### Artificial Octopus（41，不用改）

*an octopus that is made, not born* 是"X, not Y"句式，但它短、有画面，是这个作品的标题感句子，**建议保留**。

### Leviathan Absinthe（38 → 40，可选）

1. 原：*I chose the name from a long list of possible names.*
   改：删掉这句。
   为什么：没有给读者新信息（没说为什么选 Leviathan）。如果你想留，可以改成说**为什么**选它。

### Allodox（34 → 39，建议）

1. 原：*My inspiration comes from daily life. Today we have so much information and so many options, and choosing can feel stressful and scary.*
   改：*Today you face so much information and so many options that choosing can feel stressful and scary.*
   为什么：第一句很空（每个作品都可以这么说），删掉；"you" 把读者放进场景里。

### Ungreen Shop（37 → 40，可选）

1. 原：*Ungreen Shop does not pretend to be additive-free. It shows the additives on purpose, with the real amount, in a fun way.*
   改：*Ungreen Shop shows the additives on purpose, with the real amount, in a fun way.*
   为什么："不假装 X，而是 Y" 的对比套路；后半句自己就够了。

### Nuclear Tide Evolutionaries（35 → 39，建议）

1. 原：*I don't want this to become normal. So I imagined it now, as a warning.*
   改：*I imagined it now, as a warning, so that it never becomes normal.*
   为什么：两句短句 + "So" 开头的金句式收尾；合成一句，意思不变。

## About Me

### Version 1 "Warning: I Run on Toy Energy"（D1、D3 用）· 36 → 40

1. 原：*They have never been just toys to me. They are my friends, and every time I look at them, they give me a special, happy energy.*
   改：*To me they are friends, and every time I look at them, they give me a special, happy energy.*
   为什么："never been just X … They are Y" 是对比套路 + "just"。
2. 原：*When I start a project, I always think about how to turn it into something real, …*
   改：*At the start of a project, I think about how to turn it into something real, …*
   为什么："always" 是笼统的绝对词；Wh- 开头。
3. 保留：*A good toy does not need a manual to make someone smile. It speaks without a word. That is what I want from design.*
   这是首屏的大字，三句短句是有意的节奏，**建议保留**。
4. 保留：*I hope everything I design earns a place on someone's shelf.*
   是"金句"式结尾，但它就是整页的落点，**建议保留**。

### Version 2 "Let Me Guide You"（D2 用）· 35 → 40

1. 原：*… I remember its buildings and details almost like a photograph, and the next time I know exactly where to go.*
   改：*… I remember its buildings and details almost like a photograph, and the next time I know where to go.*
   为什么：删副词 "exactly"。
2. 原：*I think this is my special skill.*
   改：*This is my special skill.*
   为什么："I think" 是在打折扣，你前面已经证明了。
3. 原：*When I travel and someone takes the wrong turn, I just say, "Follow me, I know where to go."*
   改：*When I travel and someone takes the wrong turn, I say, "Follow me, I know where to go."*
   为什么：删 "just"。
4. 保留：*But I never give up the goal, only the route that is not working.*
   是对比句，但它就是这段的意思（那句中国俗语的反转），**建议保留**。
